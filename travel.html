A dojo in every country.

Three countries, about three months in each, and a mat in all of them. Judo turns out to be the fastest way into a place — you can't speak the language, but you can bow, shake hands, and get thrown by a stranger for two hours.

2022 China — without going there

You don't always have to be in the room.

One more country belongs on this page, and I've never set foot in it. The tatami the Michigan judo club trains on came from a manufacturer in China. I sourced it, negotiated the order, arranged the freight and handled the import paperwork — all of it from Ann Arbor, across a twelve-hour time difference and a language I don't speak.

It's the least glamorous thing here and probably the most useful. Getting something real done in a country you've never visited turns out to be a different skill from visiting one, and a harder one.
